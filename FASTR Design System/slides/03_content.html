Why monthly facility-level data?

Monthly facility data lets you spot service disruptions within a single quarter, rather than waiting for an annual report to confirm what every analyst already suspected.
  • Granularity — district patterns surface when national averages hide them
  • Cadence — anomalies are flagged in weeks, not years
  • Comparability — same indicators every month make trends honest
  • Decision-ready — outputs land in time to redirect supervision and supply
