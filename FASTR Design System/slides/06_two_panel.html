HMIS vs. HFA — two views, one country

Module 4 · Sources

You will hear both acronyms all week. They sound interchangeable but they answer different questions, on different cadences, with different uncertainties. Use them together.

HMISroutine, monthly, what was delivered

Facility-level records reported every cycle through DHIS2. The workhorse of rapid-cycle monitoring.

  • High cadence — monthly, full coverage of registered facilities
  • Quality varies — completeness and outliers must be checked first
  • Captures volume — visits, doses, deliveries
  • Misses readiness — staffing, stock-outs, training

HFAperiodic, sampled, what could be delivered

A survey round at sampled facilities. Slower, but adds the context HMIS alone cannot give you.

  • Annual or biennial — sampled, not census
  • Captures readiness — staffing, supplies, supervision
  • Anchors trust — calibrates the HMIS signal
  • Misses month-to-month change
