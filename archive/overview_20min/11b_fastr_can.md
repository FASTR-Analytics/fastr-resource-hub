## In summary, FASTR can ...

<div style="display: flex; gap: 1.5em; font-size: 0.82em;">
<div style="flex: 1;">

### Analyze service use and coverage trends

Across subnational areas and over time

- Has ANC coverage increased or decreased since the last DHS?
- Have there been any disruptions in typical service utilization in the past three months?
- Which regions/districts have seen an increase in skilled birth attendance? A decrease?

</div>
<div style="flex: 1;">

### Assess service readiness of PHCs

Over time & get timely feedback from facility managers

- What challenges have facilities experienced in the past three months?
- Do facilities have tracer items in stock and how much does stock fluctuate over time?
- Are facilities meeting HR guidelines?

</div>
</div>

## This data will allow you to ...

- **Identify 'red flags'** or signals that need prompt follow-up to understand root causes

- **Guide resources** to programs/services/districts that are lagging behind

- **Make course corrections** when programs/policies are not achieving intended results

- **Identify positive deviants** to learn from

**Key takeaway:** FASTR will not give you all the answers, but it can tell you where to look
