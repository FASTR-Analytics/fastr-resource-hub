## Examples of broader FASTR use cases

Strengthening data for decision making to improve RMNCAH-N

<div style="font-size: 0.72em;">

**Ghana:** Institutionalizing analysis of routine data and strengthening data use processes at national and regional levels in support of Ghana's Free PHC reform; bringing new analytics to Ghana's annual holistic health systems assessment process at national and regional levels.

**Nigeria:** Enabling timely and continuous monitoring of high priority Presidential indicators under the National Health Sector Reform Initiative at the federal and state levels. Results are feeding into quarterly performance dialogues and states' annual operating plans which govern health sector implementation.

**Guinea:** Strengthening competencies across the analyze-to-action data use cycle with DHIS2, from improving data extraction capabilities, to enhancing data interpretation and use processes on a quarterly basis.

**Tajikistan:** Tracking service readiness across Tajikistan's primary health care system, with results feeding into Tajikistan's Joint Annual Review M&E process and quarterly performance review meetings for the Healthy Nation WB/GFF co-financed project.

**Somalia:** Leveraging FASTR to revitalize and strengthen Somalia's Ministry of Health quarterly M&E review process via a multi-stakeholder M&E TWG which promotes alignment across partners. HMIS analysis – and soon the health facility survey – enable the MoH to monitor PHC performance in hard-to-reach areas and track the impact of shocks (e.g., conflict and drought) on service delivery.

</div>
