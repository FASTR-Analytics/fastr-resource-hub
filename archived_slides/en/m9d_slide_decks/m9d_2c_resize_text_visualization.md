## Step 2: Resize the text and visualization areas

In the slide preview, you will see a vertical divider between the text area (left) and the visualization area (right). You can drag this divider to resize both areas.

- **Drag left** to make the chart larger
- **Drag right** to make the text larger

**How to:** Hover over the vertical divider between text and chart until your cursor changes. Click and drag left or right to adjust the split. The text area and visualization will resize automatically.
