## What data does FASTR need?

FASTR needs **routine health facility data** — the same data that facilities report every month through DHIS2.

**Core RMNCAH-N indicators** — ANC visits, institutional deliveries, postnatal care, immunizations (BCG, Penta1, Penta3), outpatient visits

**Country-specific indicators** — each country adds indicators relevant to their priorities, such as malaria, HIV/TB, nutrition, or family planning

**Data specifications**

- **Facility level** — from each individual health facility, not pre-aggregated
- **Monthly** — one data point per facility per month
- **Over time** — ideally five years of historical data

**How it gets into FASTR** — direct import from DHIS2, or file upload (`.csv`)
