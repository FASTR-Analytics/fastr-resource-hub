## Which denominator provides more plausible estimates?

FASTR can produce coverage using several different denominators — the survey, the HMIS-derived back-calculation, the catchment population, a projection. The chart compares them side by side for the same indicator. The most plausible is the one that produces coverage values that fall in a realistic range (0–100%) and move smoothly over time, without sudden jumps or values >100% that suggest the denominator is wrong.

Use the comparison to choose the denominator best suited to your country and indicator. There is no universal answer: it depends on data quality, age of the most recent survey, and how closely catchment populations track reality.
