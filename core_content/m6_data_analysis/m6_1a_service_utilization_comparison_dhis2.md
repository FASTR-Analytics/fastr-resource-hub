## Service utilization and disruptions: comparison to DHIS2

<div style="font-size: 0.92em;">

Service utilization trends are commonly produced in DHIS2

**Main differences between DHIS2 and FASTR approach**

- FASTR adjusts for data quality (outliers and/or completeness)
- FASTR visualizes data with the percent change approach to make it easier to identify meaningful fluctuations in service delivery

**Extension of service utilization analysis** — more complex statistical approaches not available in DHIS2. Using a regression framework, we are able to:

- Account for seasonality
- Exclude unusual changes to ensure one-off events aren't influencing normal trends
- Use historical data as a baseline for context
- Detect disruptions and recovery patterns
- Quantify changes with a robust methodology as compared to just observing simple fluctuations in a trend line

This improves the ability to interpret and compare count/utilization data across national and sub-national areas without needing population denominators.

</div>
