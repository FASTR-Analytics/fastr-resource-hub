## Selecting the best denominator

**How the module selects the best denominator:**

1. **Calculate coverage** using each denominator option
2. **Compare to survey** (DHS/MICS) benchmark for that indicator
3. **Select the denominator** that produces coverage closest to survey

**Selection rules:**
- HMIS-based denominators are preferred over UN population projections
- Circular denominators are avoided (e.g., ANC1 denominator for ANC1 coverage would always equal 100%)
- Selection is made per indicator and geographic area

**In the platform:** You can visualize all denominator options in the coverage charts to see how they compare before selecting one for your analysis.
