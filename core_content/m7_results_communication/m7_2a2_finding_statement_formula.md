## Writing a finding statement

A finding statement turns raw data into a clear message. Use this formula:

**[Indicator] [trend] from [value] to [value] in [place/period]. This suggests [interpretation].**

Good example:
- "ANC4 coverage increased from 68% to 79% in Region X over the past year, suggesting improved continuity of antenatal care."

Bad examples to avoid:
- Too vague: "ANC4 is doing well"
- Too much: A long paragraph with jargon nobody remembers

Keep it to one or two sentences. If you need more, you're describing multiple findings.
