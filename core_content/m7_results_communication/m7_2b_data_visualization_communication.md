## Try it yourself

Take one FASTR output you have created — a chart, a table, or a heatmap.

- **What is the result?** Describe what the numbers show
- **What is the key takeaway?** Write the main message in one sentence

*A good test: could someone who wasn't in the room understand your takeaway without seeing the chart?*
