## Peer review on action plans

**Pair up** with another country team.

**Swap action plans** — read the other team's plan as someone who could be asked to deliver it.

**Use this checklist as you read:**

- [ ] Is the action **specific** — clear what gets done, by whom, by when?
- [ ] Is it in the team's **sphere of control or influence**, not just concern?
- [ ] Does it have a **measure of success** that someone could check next quarter?
- [ ] Is the **owner named** — a person or role, not just a department?
- [ ] Are **constraints acknowledged** — what could block delivery?

**Prepare feedback:** 2 things that are clear and actionable, 2 suggestions to strengthen.

**Report back:** Each pair shares their strongest action and one suggestion they received.
