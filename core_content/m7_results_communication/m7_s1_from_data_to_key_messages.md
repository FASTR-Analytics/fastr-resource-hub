## From data to key messages

### Results vs key takeaways

| Results | Key takeaways |
|---------|---------------|
| What the analysis found | What the results are telling us |
| Many, complex, technical | Few, simple, actionable |
| Data quality scores, coverage numbers, utilization trends | Why it matters — the "so what" |

**Example:**

**Result:** *"ANC1 visits increased 15% in Q3 then dropped 12% in Q1, before stabilizing from mid-2023."*

**Key takeaway:** *"Service delivery has stabilized since mid-2023, suggesting strengthened systems — providing a foundation for sustained antenatal care coverage."*
