## From data to key messages

Your FASTR analysis produces many outputs — scores, charts, trends. Your audience needs **a few clear messages** they can act on.

**The shift:** move from *what the data shows* to *what it means and what to do about it*

| Don't say | Say instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| "ANC1 visits increased 15% in Q3 then dropped 12% in Q1, before stabilizing from mid-2023" | "Service delivery has **stabilized since mid-2023** — a foundation for sustained antenatal care coverage" |
| "The data quality score for District X is 62%" | "District X has **significant data quality gaps** that need to be addressed before relying on its reported numbers" |
| "Penta3 coverage is 78% nationally" | "**One in five children** is not completing their vaccination schedule" |

*For each result, ask: **"So what?"** — that answer is your key message.*
