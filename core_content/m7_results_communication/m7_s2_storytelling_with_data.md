## Storytelling with data

Translate data into compelling narratives that guide decision-making.

### Elements of a data story

- **Plot**: What questions does the data answer?
- **Setting**: What is the context?
- **Audience**: Who needs to hear this?
- **Issue**: What is the main health challenge?
- **Implication**: What actions or recommendations follow?

### Tips
- Lead with the takeaway, not the methodology
- Use visually clear charts — one message per visualization
- Tailor the story to your audience: a minister needs different framing than a district health officer
