## Choosing the right report format

### Key considerations
- **Audience** — Who will use the report?
- **Purpose** — Inform, persuade, summarize, or analyze?
- **Level of detail** — High-level summary vs in-depth analysis
- **Accessibility** — How will the audience access and use it?

| Audience | Format |
|----------|--------|
| Government officials and policymakers | Brief slide deck + executive summary |
| Technical experts and researchers | Full report + data annex |
| District and facility managers | Dashboard + action-oriented summary |

*Match the format to how your audience makes decisions*
