## Why integrate HMIS and HFA surveys?

Integration between HMIS data (DHIS2) and HFA survey data is **necessary** to measure priority health indicators.

<div class="columns-3" style="margin-top:1.5em">

<div>
<div class="data-pill data-pill-deep-green" style="font-size:0.7em">1</div>

### DHIS2
*HMIS data on RMNCAH-N services*

Routine, monthly data. Captures **what was delivered** — visits, doses, deliveries.
</div>

<div style="text-align:center; font-size:2em; color:var(--fastr-gold); align-self:center">⇄</div>

<div>
<div class="data-pill data-pill-green" style="font-size:0.7em">2</div>

### HFA surveys
*Health facility data*

Quarterly phone surveys. Captures **what can be delivered** — staffing, supplies, infrastructure.
</div>

</div>

<div class="callout-footer">Triangulating data lets us measure what really matters for maternal and newborn health.</div>
