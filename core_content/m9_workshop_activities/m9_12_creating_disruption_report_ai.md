## Activity: Creating a disruption report with the AI Assistant

![hands-on h:40](../../resources/icons/hands_on.svg) **Step by step:**

1. Open the **Slide Decks** tab → Create a new slide deck (e.g., "Disruption Report for {country}")
2. Open the **AI Assistant** → Start a new conversation
3. Open the **Prompt Library** → Select **"Prompt 1: FASTR Disruptions Report"**
4. Review the prompt text that appears in the input field
5. **Run the prompt** → The AI will ask for: country name, analysis time period, and report subtitle
6. The AI will propose **indicator groupings** — review and confirm with your team
7. The AI builds the report slide by slide (5-10 minutes)

**Tip:** Let the AI complete the full slide generation before clicking on any slide — clicking will interrupt the process.
