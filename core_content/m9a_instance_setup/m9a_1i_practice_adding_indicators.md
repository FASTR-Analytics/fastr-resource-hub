## Practice: adding indicators as a team

We have prepared a spreadsheet of DHIS2 indicators already found and selected for the platform — the ones you need to reproduce your reports and bulletins. It has one tab per working group.

**How we will work — one screen at a time.** To avoid conflicts and errors, indicators are added from a single computer with the screen shared, and each group takes a turn.

1. **Take turns** — the facilitator shares their screen; each group takes the lead in turn
2. **Add your indicators** — open your group's tab, add your assigned indicators as shown in the demonstration, then hand over to the next group
3. **One data update** — once every group has entered its indicators, import the data once, from this computer
