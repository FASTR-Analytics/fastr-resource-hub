## Practice: adding indicators as a team

We have prepared a spreadsheet of DHIS2 indicators already found and selected for the platform, the ones you need to reproduce your reports and bulletins. It has one tab per working group.

**How we will work: one screen at a time.** To avoid duplicates, indicators are added from a single computer with the screen shared, and each group takes a turn.

1. **Take turns**: the facilitator shares the screen; each group leads in turn
2. **Add your indicators**: open your group's tab, add your assigned indicators as shown, then hand over
3. **One import**: once every group has finished, run one DHIS2 import from this computer, then generate one results package
