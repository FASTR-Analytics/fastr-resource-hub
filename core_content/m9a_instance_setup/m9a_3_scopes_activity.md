## Activity: Scopes, not projects

**In this hands-on session, you will:**

- Create a scope on the **Scopes** page: one region and a range of years, with a label the team will recognize
- Attach it to a product: ⋮ menu → **Results package and scope…**, and watch every figure refilter
- Decide what belongs where: a scope limits what a product **shows**, **Manage access** limits who can **open** it

*Projects are gone: their package moved to the product, their restriction became a scope, their team became folder access.*
