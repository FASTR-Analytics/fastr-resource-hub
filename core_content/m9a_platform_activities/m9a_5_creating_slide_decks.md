## Activity: Creating slide decks

![hands-on h:40](../../resources/icons/hands_on.svg) **Step-by-step**

**Create your slide deck:** Click your folder → Click **+ Create slide deck** → Name it → Click **Save**

**Add your first slide:** Click **Add slide** → Select **Content slide**

---

## Adding visualizations to slide decks

**I do:** Facilitator demonstrates adding a visualization to the slide deck by selecting it from the available visualizations and inserting it into the presentation.

**We do:** Together, we add a previously created visualization into the slide deck.

**You do:** Practice adding a visualization you already created into your own presentation.

---

## Adding visualizations using AI Assistant

**I do:** Facilitator demonstrates prompting the AI Assistant to show an existing visualization, then uses the buttons in the AI chat preview to add it to the slide deck.

**You do:** Prompt the AI Assistant to show a visualization you have already created, then add it to your own presentation.

**Prompt:** *"Display the quarterly change in service volume visualization"*
