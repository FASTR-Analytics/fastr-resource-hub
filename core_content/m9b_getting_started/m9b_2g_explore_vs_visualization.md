## Explore or a visualization?

<div class="columns">
<div>

### Explore

- For **looking**
- Every metric, every view, at once
- Filters only: no title, no colours, no layout
- Nothing saved, the same for everyone
- Cannot go into a deck or a report

</div>
<div>

### A visualization

- For **keeping and sharing**
- You choose the metric, the filters, the shape
- Has a row on the Products page, in your folder
- Copied into decks and reports
- The AI assistant can edit it

</div>
</div>

**The rule:** when a view in Explore answers your question, build it as a visualization. That is the next module.
