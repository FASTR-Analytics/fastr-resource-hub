## Activity: Take a first look with Explore

**In this hands-on session, you will:**

- Open **Explore** in the side navigation: every computed analysis, with nothing to create
- Read one indicator in the **Table**, then watch it **Over time**, then try the **Map**
- Switch module under **Supporting analyses** (for example **Coverage estimates**)
- Bring back three numbers to the room

*Handout: Take a first look with Explore. Explore is for looking; a visualization is for keeping and sharing.*
