## From manual reporting to AI-assisted reports

**Over the past months, we've worked with you to create reports.** This process was done manually and typically involved:

1. Create visualizations for data quality assessment, service utilization, and service coverage
2. Add the visualization to a slide deck
3. Write the interpretation
4. Write the introduction, executive summary, and other narrative sections

**The goal now is to enable you to do this yourself by leveraging the AI.** In this session, we will reflect on how reports were created manually, revisit the key steps, and use the AI to support and streamline the same reporting workflow.
