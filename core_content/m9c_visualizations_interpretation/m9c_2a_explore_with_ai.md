## Explore with the AI Assistant

**Follow these three steps. You can type or copy the prompts exactly as shown.**

**1. Try this prompt**

*"Show ANC1 service utilization by region over the last 12 months"*

This works because it tells the AI: what to show (ANC1), where (by region), when (last 12 months).

**2. Now change one thing**

- Change the indicator: *"Show BCG dose by region over the last 12 months"*
- Change the time: *"Show ANC1 by region for 2023 and 2024"*
- Change the level: *"Show ANC1 by district over the last 12 months"*

Pick one and try it. Notice how changing one word changes the output.

**3. Ask a follow-up**

After getting a result, try asking: *"Show this as a bar chart"* | *"Which region had the biggest change?"* | *"Add Penta1 to the comparison"* | *"Explain what this means"*

You don't need to start over — just keep the conversation going. Save any visualizations you like to your own folder.
