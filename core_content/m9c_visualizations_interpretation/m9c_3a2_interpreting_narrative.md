## Example narrative: ANC1 and ANC4 trends

*"Across the country, the number of women attending their first antenatal care visit (ANC1) has gone up and down over time, ranging from about 49,000 to 61,000. There was a noticeable drop of 13% in mid-2024, but numbers bounced back quickly. What's more interesting is what's happening with fourth visits (ANC4) — for almost two years, these stayed flat at around 25,000, but then jumped sharply in early-to-mid 2025 (up 19%, then another 14%). This suggests that more women are completing their recommended visits, not just starting care. It's worth looking into what's behind this change — whether it's due to new programs, policy changes, or improvements in how the data is being recorded."*
