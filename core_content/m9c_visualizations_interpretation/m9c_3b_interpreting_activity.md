## Activity: Interpreting visualizations in slide decks

**I do, we do, you do**

**I do:** Facilitator creates a slide by inserting a visualization and adding an interpretation — considering the indicator, values, context, and conclusions

**We do:** Together, we interpret a visualization and add the interpretation to a slide deck

**You do:** Interpret a visualization of your choice and add it to your presentation

*We are in the Slide Decks tab*
