## The AI will propose indicator groupings

Next, the AI reads your country's data and discovers which health indicators are available. It then groups them into categories and asks you to review.

**What the AI does:**

1. Scans all indicators available in your country's platform
2. Groups them into categories (e.g. Immunization, ANC, Malaria)
3. Presents the full list and asks: do these groupings make sense?
4. Flags anything unusual

**Example groupings the AI might propose:**

- Antenatal Care — ANC1, ANC4, ANC in first trimester
- Deliveries & PNC — Facility delivery, Skilled birth attendance
- Immunization — BCG, Penta 1, Penta 3
- Family Planning — New FP users, FP clients counseled
- Malaria — Malaria positive cases, Malaria treatment
- HIV/TB — HIV treated, TB new and relapse
- OPD — Outpatient department visits
- Nutrition — Vitamin A supplementation, Severe acute malnutrition

**Your team decides:**

- ✓ Do these groupings make sense for your country context?
- ✎ Would you like to merge, split, or rename any groups?
- ✕ Are there indicators to exclude? (e.g. low-volume or not a priority)
