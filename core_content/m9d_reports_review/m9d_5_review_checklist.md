## Review checklist: Verifying the AI output

After the AI generates your report, review each section as a team using this checklist.

**General (all prompts)**

- [ ] All slides were generated — none missing or incomplete
- [ ] Country name is correct and consistent across all slides
- [ ] Time period is correct on all charts
- [ ] No placeholder text like [COUNTRY] or [VERIFY] remaining
- [ ] Back page (FASTR initiative link) is the very last slide

**Prompt 1: Disruptions Report**

- [ ] Cover slide has correct country, subtitle, and date
- [ ] Indicator groupings match what you confirmed
- [ ] Each group has its own analysis slide
- [ ] Charts are loading and showing data (not blank)
- [ ] Interpretations match what the chart shows
- [ ] Slide titles are analytical, not just indicator names

**Final:** Read through all interpretations as a team — does anything sound wrong or exaggerated?
