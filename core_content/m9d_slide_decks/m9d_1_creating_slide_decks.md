## Activity: Creating slide decks

**Step-by-step**

**Create your slide deck:** Click your folder → Click **+ Create slide deck** → Name it → Click **Save**

**Add your first slide:** Click **Add slide** → Select **Content slide**

---

## Activity: Adding visualizations to slide decks manually

**I do, we do, you do**

**I do:** Facilitator demonstrates adding a visualization to the slide deck by selecting it from the available visualizations and inserting it into the presentation using the slide-deck editing function (reusing the visualization created earlier).

**We do:** Together, we add one of the previously created visualizations into the slide deck by selecting and inserting it into the presentation.

**You do:** Practice adding a visualization you already created into your own presentation.

*We are in the Slide Decks tab*

## Activity: Adding visualizations to slide deck using AI Assistant

**I do, you do**

**I do:** Facilitator demonstrates prompting the AI Assistant to show an existing visualization, then uses the buttons in the AI chat preview to add it to the slide deck.

**We do:** Together, we prompt the AI Assistant to display a previously created visualization and practice adding it to the slide deck using the buttons in the AI chat preview, discussing placement and layout as a group.

**You do:** Prompt the AI Assistant to show a visualization you have already created, then add it to your own presentation using the buttons in the AI chat preview.

**Prompt:** *"Display the quarterly change in service volume visualization"*

*We are in the Slide Decks tab*
