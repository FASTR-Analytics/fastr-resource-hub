## Activity: Add a visualisation to a slide using AI

**What you'll learn**
How to ask the AI to place a specific chart on a specific slide. The AI does not always pick the chart you meant, so you check the result before keeping it.

**What you'll practice**
- Prompt the AI to add a viz to a target slide
- Confirm it picked the right chart, period and adjusted data
- Refine the prompt if it didn't
- Save the deck
