## Demo: How to edit a slide

**What you'll see**
The five steps for editing a slide in FASTR. You open the editor, add a visualization block, split the slide into a chart area and a text area, resize each area, and save.

**What to watch for**
- The cursor change on the resize divider
- Which fixes belong in the editor vs back in the Visualisations tab
- How the chart and text reflow after a resize
