## What a FASTR disruption report looks like

Before you build one, here is the format the AI will produce:

| # | Section | Content |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | **Cover page** | Title, country, quarter, date |
| 2 | **Introduction** | Context and purpose of the analysis |
| 3 | **Methodology** | How results were produced + selected indicators |
| 4 | **Section 1: Service utilization** | Disruptions and surpluses by indicator group |
| 5 | **Section 2: Coverage** | National and subnational coverage estimates |
| 6 | **Conclusions** | Key messages and next steps |

Each section contains **2 to 5 slides** with charts + interpretation.
