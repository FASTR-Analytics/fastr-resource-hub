## Time to build your final output

**How we will work**

We will explain how to prompt the AI, and explore the pre-built prompts together.

One person drives the report creation. Pick a team member to run the prompts. Everyone else guides and contributes.

Then the whole team edits. Once the report is generated, everyone joins in to review, refine and polish.

We will regroup on styling before you finalize. We will demonstrate how to format text and change the theme color on your decks.

**In this session, you will use the AI to generate a disruption report for your country using the most up-to-date data; then refine it into a finished presentation.**
