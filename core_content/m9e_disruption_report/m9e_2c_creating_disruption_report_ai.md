## The AI Assistant will ask for your report details

After you send the prompt, the AI will ask you a series of questions before it starts building the presentation. Your team's driver should type the answers directly in the chat.

1. **Analysis time period** — provide a start and end date for the data you want to analyze (e.g., "January 2023 to September 2025")
2. **Cover subtitle** — the AI will offer options like "Q3 2025", "2025 Annual", or you can type your own
3. **Indicator groupings** — the AI discovers what health indicators are available in your country's platform, groups them into categories (e.g. Immunization, Antenatal Care), and asks you to review and adjust
4. **Mortality indicators** — the AI will ask separately whether to include mortality indicators (e.g. maternal deaths, stillbirths), since these require different interpretation

Now run the prompt. Click **Run in current chat**.
