## The AI will propose indicator groupings

The AI reads your country's data and discovers which health indicators are available. It then groups them into categories and asks you to review.

<div class="columns">
<div>

**What the AI does:**

1. Scans all indicators available in your country's platform
2. Groups them into categories (e.g. Immunization, ANC, Malaria)
3. Presents the full list and asks: do these groupings make sense?
4. Flags anything unusual

</div>
<div>

**Your team decides:**

- ✓ Do these groupings make sense for your country context?
- ✎ Would you like to merge, split, or rename any groups?
- ✕ Are there indicators to exclude?

</div>
</div>

**Example groupings:** Antenatal Care (ANC1 = 1st visit, ANC4 = 4th visit) · Deliveries & Postnatal Care · Immunization (BCG, Penta 1, Penta 3) · Family Planning · Malaria · HIV/TB · Outpatient Department · Nutrition
