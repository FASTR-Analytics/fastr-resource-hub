## Review checklist: Verifying the AI output

After the AI generates your report, review each section as a team.

<div class="columns">
<div>

**General (all prompts)**

- [ ] All slides generated — none missing
- [ ] Country name correct and consistent
- [ ] Time period correct on all charts
- [ ] No placeholder text like [COUNTRY] remaining
- [ ] Back page is the very last slide

</div>
<div>

**Disruptions report**

- [ ] Cover slide has correct country, subtitle, date
- [ ] Indicator groupings match what you confirmed
- [ ] Each group has its own analysis slide
- [ ] Charts are loading and showing data
- [ ] Interpretations match the charts
- [ ] Slide titles are analytical, not just names

</div>
</div>

*Read through all interpretations as a team — does anything sound wrong or exaggerated?*
