## Writing analytical slide titles

When you refine the AI's draft, the title is the first thing to check. **A good title is a finding, not a description.** The reader should understand the main message without reading the rest of the slide.

| | Title |
|---|-------|
| ❌ | ANC1, ANC4, and early ANC1 results |
| ✅ | ANC1 remains stable, but ANC4 shows a persistent deficit |

**Each interpretation should answer three questions:**

- What you **see** in the chart (trend, numbers)
- What it **means** for the program
- What **questions** it raises

If a slide's title is just an indicator name, rewrite it. If the interpretation doesn't answer the three questions, ask the AI to refine it — or rewrite yourself.
