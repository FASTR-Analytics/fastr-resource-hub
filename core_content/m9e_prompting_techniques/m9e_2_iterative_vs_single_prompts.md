## Exercise: AI & iterative conversation

**In this exercise, we are building a short data quality report step by step using the AI**

**Type these prompts into the AI (in order):**

1. *"Can you help me understand the quality of our 2024 data? I'd like a 3-slide summary I can share with my team."*
2. *"Let's focus on overall quality, regional differences, and priorities for improvement."*
3. *"Which region has the weakest data quality? Highlight that."*
4. *"What should we do about this? Make the final slide more actionable."*

---

## Exercise: AI & single prompt

**In this exercise, we are generating the same report using one well-structured prompt**

**Type this prompt into the AI:**

*"Build a 3-slide data quality report for 2024 covering (1) overall status, (2) regional comparison, and (3) recommendations."*

---

## Iterative vs single prompts: What did you notice?

- How did the outputs differ between the iterative prompts and the single prompt?
- Which approach felt easier or more natural?
- Where did the AI make assumptions that needed clarification?

**Single vs. iterative prompts:**

- Both approaches use the same underlying data and methods
- Iterative prompting gives more control and room to refine
- A strong single prompt can be faster when your goal is clear
