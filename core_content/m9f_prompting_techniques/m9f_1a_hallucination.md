## When the AI makes things up

A confident-sounding answer isn't always a correct answer. The AI is trying to be helpful, and when it doesn't know something it will often **fill in a plausible-sounding answer** rather than say "I don't know" — a number, a name, a date.

The good news: it's easy to spot the moments when this matters most, and easy to check.

**Things worth a second look:**

- A specific number with no source attached
- A round-sounding figure ("about a million", "roughly half") that should be exact
- A name, date, or quote you don't recognize

**A simple check:** ask the AI *"where did this come from?"* and trace it back to your data or a document you uploaded. If it can't show you, treat it as a draft you still need to confirm.

Your job isn't to police the AI — it's to **bring the judgment** the AI doesn't have.
