## Two ways to ask for help

The AI is great at two different jobs. One is more reliable than the other — and knowing which you're asking for makes a big difference.

<div class="columns">
<div>

### "Help me improve what I wrote"

You give the AI your text. It tightens the wording, fixes typos, adjusts the tone for your audience.

**The facts come from you** — so the answer stays grounded in what you already know.

Best for: polishing a draft, shortening for a one-pager, simplifying for a wider audience.

</div>
<div>

### "Help me draft something new"

You give the AI a topic. It produces a first version — an outline, a paragraph, a starting point.

**The AI is filling in details** — so a careful read at the end is part of the work.

Best for: brainstorming, structuring an outline, getting unstuck on a blank page.

</div>
</div>

> **Same idea, different starting points.** If the AI starts from your draft, less to check. If it starts from a blank page, more to check.
