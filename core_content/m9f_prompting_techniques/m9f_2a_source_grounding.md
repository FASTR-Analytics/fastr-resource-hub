## Hand the AI the right materials

If you asked a colleague to write a report section, you'd hand them the documents you want them to use. The AI works the same way.

By default, the AI draws on everything it remembers — including things that are outdated or wrong for your context. Telling it which materials to use is the simplest way to get a trustworthy answer.

**Three phrases you can drop into any prompt:**

- *"Use only the data and documents I have given you to answer."*
- *"If the answer isn't in what I provided, say so — don't fill in from elsewhere."*
- *"Quote from the source when you can, and tell me where it came from."*

**In FASTR you're already covered for your country data.** The AI Assistant only looks at your country's data — not the internet, not other countries. When you bring in an outside document (a PDF you upload, an excerpt you paste), the same idea applies: point the AI at it and ask for quotes.
