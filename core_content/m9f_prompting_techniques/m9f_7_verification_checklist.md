## Give the AI's answer a quick read

Before you share what the AI wrote, give it the same read you'd give a colleague's draft. Most of the work is fast — you're just checking the things that matter most.

<div class="columns">
<div>

**The things worth checking yourself**

- Numbers and percentages
- The main recommendation or finding
- Anything that could be wrong and *would matter* if it were

Open your data or source and look. This is usually a one-minute job per number.

</div>
<div>

**The things you can ask the AI to check**

For a claim tied to a document you gave it:

*"Can you point me to where this comes from in the source? Quote the passage."*

If the AI can show you, it's grounded. If it can't, you know to take a closer look.

</div>
</div>

**One last pass before you share:**

- Numbers in the summary match the numbers in the body
- Acronyms are introduced once, then used the same way throughout
- No two sections contradict each other

> **You stay in charge.** The AI helps you move faster — your read at the end is what makes it ready to share.
