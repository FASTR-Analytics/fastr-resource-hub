## Step 1: Create a report

<!-- PLACEHOLDER — screenshot of Reports tab + Create button -->

1. Go to the **Reports** tab
2. Click **Create a report**
3. Give it a title: "Disruption monitoring — [Country] — Q4 2025"

---

## Step 2: Add a slide

<!-- PLACEHOLDER — screenshot of slide creation interface -->

1. Click **Add a slide**
2. Select the visualizations to include (disruption charts, coverage, etc.)
3. Group indicators that tell the same story

**Example**: long-acting and short-acting contraceptive methods together, because they show the same declining trend.

---

## Step 3: Write the interpretation

For each slide, you need to write:

**1. The title** — the main message in one sentence

> *Prolonged decline in contraceptive methods, especially short-acting*

**2. The interpretation text** — describe:
- What you **see** in the charts (trends, numbers)
- What it **means** for the program
- The **questions** it raises

**Tip**: look at the red and green areas. Red areas = volumes below expectations. Start there.

---

## How to write a good slide title

The title should be a **finding**, not a description. Compare:

| | Title |
|---|-------|
| ❌ | ANC1, ANC4, and early ANC1 results |
| ✅ | ANC1 remains stable, but ANC4 shows a persistent deficit |

The reader should understand the message **without reading the rest of the slide**.
