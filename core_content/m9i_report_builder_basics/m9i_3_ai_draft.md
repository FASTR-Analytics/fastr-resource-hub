## Speed up with the AI assistant

Writing interpretation takes time. The AI assistant can help you **get started faster**.

**How:**

1. Go to the **prompt library**
2. Choose the "Quarterly report draft" prompt
3. The AI looks at your visualizations and proposes interpretation text

<!-- PLACEHOLDER — screenshot of prompt library -->

The AI generates a **draft** — titles + text for each slide. Your job: verify, correct, complete.

---

## AI proposes, you decide

The AI draft is a starting point. **You are responsible for the final product.**

**What the AI does well:**
- Describe visible trends in the charts
- Identify disruption and surplus areas
- Structure the text (title + interpretation)

**What you need to add:**
- Local context (strike, stock-out, vaccination campaign...)
- Programmatic nuance (is this a real problem or a data artifact?)
- Action recommendations

> At the Dakar workshop, you'll learn to use the AI to generate a full report in minutes. Today, we start by doing it manually.
