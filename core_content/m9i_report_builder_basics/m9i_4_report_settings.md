## Report settings

Once your report is created, you can adjust its appearance. Everything lives in the **Settings** panel.

### General

- Update the report **name**
- Delete the report (if needed)

### Style

- Slide **color**
- Edit **background details**

### Logos

- Add slide **logos**, from uploaded files

### Page details

- Show **page numbers**

> ⚠ Make sure to **save** the settings after each change — otherwise your edits will be lost.
