## Expanding your report with more prompts

Once the basic disruption report is generated, you can ask the AI to **add more sections**.

<div class="columns">

<div>

### For regional analysis
**Prompt 2 — Regional disruption analysis**

The AI generates **one slide per region** showing observed service volumes vs expected.

*Select it from the prompt library.*

</div>

<div>

### For data quality
**Prompt 3 — Data quality assessment**

The AI adds an **appendix** with visualisations and interpretation of data quality.

*Select it from the prompt library.*

</div>

</div>

> You can select one or both. The AI will add these sections to your existing report.
