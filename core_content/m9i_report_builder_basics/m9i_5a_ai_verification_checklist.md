## Verification checklist — AI output

After the AI generates your report, review each section **as a team** using this checklist.

<div class="columns">

<div>

### Prompt 2 — Regional analysis

- ☐ The summary includes **all** subnational areas
- ☐ **One slide per area** — none missing
- ☐ Area **names are spelled correctly**
- ☐ Interpretations reference the **right area**

</div>

<div>

### Prompt 3 — Data quality

- ☐ The appendix is **numbered correctly** (1 or 2)
- ☐ Slides on **completeness, outliers, and consistency** are present
- ☐ The **colour code** is logical (green = good, red = alert)
- ☐ Areas or indicators with **poor quality** are flagged

</div>

</div>

<div class="warning">

**Final check**: read every interpretation as a team — does anything sound wrong or exaggerated?

</div>
