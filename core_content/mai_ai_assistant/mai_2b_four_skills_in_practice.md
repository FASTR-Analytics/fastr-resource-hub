## Four habits make working with AI actually work

The AI is a fast, capable colleague — but you're the one who knows the country, the data, and what *good* looks like. Four habits make that partnership work, the same habits that make you good at any kind of teamwork.

<div class="columns">
<div>

### Discernment
*Know what good looks like — before and after.*

**Before** you ask: picture the answer you want. If you can't describe what *good* looks like, you can't tell whether what the AI gave you is any good.

**After:** read the answer carefully. Would you be happy to put your name on it?

</div>
<div>

### Description
*Brief them clearly — and keep the conversation going.*

You wouldn't drop a one-line request on a new colleague and expect a finished report. You'd explain the audience, the data, the format — and adjust as they show you drafts.

A prompt is not one perfect sentence. It's a conversation.

</div>
</div>

<div class="columns">
<div>

### Delegation
*Hand over the right-sized task.*

You wouldn't ask someone on their first day to write the whole report. You'd start small — *"draft the methodology section"* — and build from there.

Break the work into steps. One thing at a time.

</div>
<div>

### Diligence
*You sign the work.*

The colleague helps you draft, but **your name goes on it**. Numbers, sources, recommendations — give them a look before you share.

If you wouldn't sign it, it's not done.

</div>
</div>
