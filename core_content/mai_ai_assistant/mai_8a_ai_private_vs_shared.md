## Private vs shared on team projects

<div class="columns">
<div>

**Private to you:**

- Your conversation with the AI
- Questions you ask and answers you receive

Other team members cannot see what you're exploring.

</div>
<div>

**Shared with team:**

- The underlying data (everyone sees the same health data)
- Saved charts in the project library
- Slide decks and reports you create and save
- Project settings and analysis results

Everyone can see saved content.

</div>
</div>
