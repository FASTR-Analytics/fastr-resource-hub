## Objective

Rapid-cycle analytics accelerate RMNCAH-N progress by strengthening the **systematic and timely use of data** for decision-making.

**What is FASTR?**

An approach to catalyze continuous cycles of **analyze, learn, strengthen, and act** to encourage the systematic use of data in decision-making.

**How do we get there?**

FASTR offers a set of **rigorous, rapid, and low-cost** approaches for monitoring and evaluating primary healthcare systems, accompanied by capacity strengthening.
