## FASTR approach to RMNCAH-N monitoring

**Quarterly analysis of DHIS2 data**, focused on national priority indicators:

- **Build sustainable tools** so stakeholders can generate the right analyses and visualizations, at the right time, on their indicators of interest
- **Combine analysis with capacity strengthening** for sustainability and institutionalization

### Data triangulation

DHIS2 and health facility data must be **integrated** to answer key questions:
- DHIS2 analysis answers questions about **quarterly results** (coverage, trends)
- Facility phone surveys deepen the **"why"** (readiness, quality)
- Household surveys add **demand-side** data (access, experience)
