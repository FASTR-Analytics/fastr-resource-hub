## Data sources at a glance

FASTR brings together multiple data sources for a complete picture of health service performance:

- **DHIS2 / HMIS** — Service delivery and coverage *(Monthly)*
- **Surveys** — DHS, MICS: population-level outcomes *(Every 3–5 years)*
- **Facility assessments** — Service readiness and quality *(Periodic)*
- **Administrative data** — Population estimates and health workforce *(Annual)*

FASTR brings these together for a **complete picture** of health service performance.
