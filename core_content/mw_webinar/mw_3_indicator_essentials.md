## Key RMNCAH-N indicators

<style scoped>
li { margin-bottom: 4px; }
</style>

- **Reproductive** — Family planning users, contraceptive prevalence
- **Maternal** — ANC visits, facility deliveries, postnatal care
- **Newborn** — Early breastfeeding, neonatal care
- **Child** — Penta3, measles vaccination, ORS treatment
- **Nutrition** — Growth monitoring, Vitamin A supplementation

These indicators form the foundation of every FASTR analysis.
