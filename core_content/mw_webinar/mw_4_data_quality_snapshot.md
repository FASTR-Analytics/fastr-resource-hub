## Data quality at a glance

Before analyzing data, we check **three dimensions**:

| Dimension | Question | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Completeness** | Are facilities reporting? | Reporting rates by period |
| **Outliers** | Any extreme values? | Statistical thresholds |
| **Consistency** | Do numbers add up? | Cross-indicator checks |

A quick quality assessment tells us **how much we can trust our data** before drawing conclusions.
