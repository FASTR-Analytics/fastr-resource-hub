## AI assistant

**What it does:**
- Answers questions about **your data**
- Creates visualizations from natural language
- Helps interpret trends and flag issues
- Generates draft narratives for reports

**What it does NOT do:**
- Make decisions for you
- Replace your expertise
- Access data outside your project
- Search the internet

The AI is an **accelerator** — you stay in control.
