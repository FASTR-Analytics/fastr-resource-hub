## Service disruptions as one FASTR use case

FASTR is designed to support **country-defined policy questions** using routine and survey data. Service disruptions analysis is one use case within the broader FASTR work program.

**Different entry points for different countries:**

- **Disruptions-first:** Sierra Leone, Burkina Faso, Zambia began with service continuity monitoring
- **Other priorities first:** Nigeria, Somalia initiated FASTR for different questions, with disruptions as complementary analysis

**Countries engage with FASTR to answer policy-relevant questions including:**

- Monitoring service continuity and recovery
- Identifying geographic or service-specific vulnerabilities
- Informing prioritization, planning, and policy dialogue
