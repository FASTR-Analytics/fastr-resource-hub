# GFF demo -- internal summary

## What's happening

Ashley is demoing FASTR live to the **GFF Trust Fund Committee** (all GFF donors) in a 5-7 minute slot. Peter frames it, then Ashley takes over. The goal: show donors that FASTR can rapidly turn routine health data into actionable analysis -- and that it can start connecting service delivery data with financing data.

## What we prepared

- This demonstrates: editability, speed, audit trail
