# Before you begin

<p class="meta-line"><strong>Activity</strong> · <strong>Instance Setup</strong> · <strong>~5 min</strong></p>

## What you need in hand

- ☐ Your **FASTR Data Prep Checklist** (filled in — *Indicator mapping template* sheet). [Download the blank template](https://github.com/FASTR-Analytics/fastr-resource-hub/raw/main/resources/checklists/FASTR_data_prep_checklist_en.xlsx)
- ☐ Your DHIS2 instance URL (the one your team uses, e.g., `https://hmis.yourcountry.gov`)
- ☐ Your DHIS2 username
- ☐ Your DHIS2 password
- ☐ A browser open on a stable network

## What's coming next

You'll work through four handouts in order:

1. **Import facility structure** — pull your country's admin hierarchy and facility list from DHIS2
2. **Import and map indicators** — define what to track and link them to DHIS2 indicator names
3. **Import HMIS data** — pull the actual data values
4. **Verify and explore** — confirm everything is loaded correctly

## About DHIS2 credentials

There is no separate "connect" step. The first time you trigger an import (admin areas, indicators, or HMIS data), the platform shows a small form:

- DHIS2 URL
- DHIS2 Username
- DHIS2 Password
- ☐ Save credentials for this session

> **Tip:** Tick **Save credentials for this session** on the first prompt. The platform remembers your DHIS2 connection until you log out — no need to re-enter for the next imports.

## How to use these handouts

Each handout starts with a "Before you start" section listing what should already be done. Read it first — don't skip ahead, the order matters.

## What's next

Move on to **Import facility structure**.
