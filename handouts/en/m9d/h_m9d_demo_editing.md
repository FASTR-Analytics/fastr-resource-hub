# Editor walkthrough

<p class="meta-line"><strong>Demo</strong> · <strong>Facilitator notes</strong> · <strong>Slide Decks</strong> · <strong>~5 min</strong></p>

## What you're demonstrating

A short tour of the slide editor — three things participants need to find: how to open the editor, how to resize the chart-vs-text split, and where the title/content/footer tabs live. Keep it under 5 minutes. The participant activity *Edit and finalise* immediately follows this demo.

## Setup checklist

- ☐ A slide deck with at least one content slide (chart + text) loaded
- ☐ Browser zoom set to ~100% so participants can see UI clearly
- ☐ Screen-share working if remote

## Demo script

### Step 1 — Open the editor

- **Do:** Click any slide in the deck's slide list.
- **Say:** *"Clicking the slide opens the editor. The preview is on the right; the editing options are on the left."*
- **Watch for:** Participants ask "where's the editor?" — answer: it's not a separate window, it's the right-hand pane that just appeared.

### Step 2 — Resize the chart and text areas

- **Do:** Hover the mouse over the vertical divider between the chart (right) and text (left). Cursor changes to a resize handle. Drag left or right.
- **Say:** *"This is how you give the chart more space, or give the text more space. Drag left to enlarge the chart, drag right to enlarge the text. Both resize automatically."*
- **Watch for:** Participants who didn't notice the cursor change — point it out explicitly.

### Step 3 — Other edits available

- **Do:** Click through the editor tabs: **Slide** → header/date fields. **Content** → text edits. **Header/Footer** → date and footer.
- **Say:** *"Four things you can edit here: the headline (Slide tab), the interpretation text (Content tab), the date and footer (Header/Footer tab), or you can ask the AI to rewrite a slide."*
- **Watch for:** Participants ask whether they should edit text directly or go back to AI — answer: small fixes here, structural rewrites go back to AI.

## Common questions

> **Q: Can I undo a resize?**
> A: Yes — undo with Ctrl/Cmd-Z, or drag the divider back. Edits are not destructive.

> **Q: Does the editor save automatically?**
> A: Yes. The Save button at the top of the deck is a manual safety net for major changes.

> **Q: Can I change the slide order in the editor?**
> A: No — slide ordering is in the deck view, not inside an individual slide editor. Drag slides in the slide list.

## Backup plan

> If the demo platform is unavailable: walk through screenshots from the *Add a visualisation + text* reference handout. The flow can still be described — the click-by-click isn't the only way to teach this.

## After the demo

Hand off to participants for the **Edit and finalise your slides** activity (15-20 min). Circulate during the activity — the most common stumble is forgetting to click into a slide to open the editor.
