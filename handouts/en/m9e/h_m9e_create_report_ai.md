# Create a disruption report with the AI

<p class="meta-line"><strong>Activity</strong> · <strong>Disruption Report</strong> · <strong>~60 min</strong></p>

<div class="p1-grid">
<aside class="p1-sidebar">

<p class="sb-label">Before you start</p>

- ☐ You're signed in and on your country's project
- ☐ You've built a report by hand once (the previous activity)
- ☐ One team member is the **driver** (types in the chat)

<p class="sb-label">What it makes</p>

A full disruption report — cover, methodology, and one analysis slide per indicator group, each with a chart and a written interpretation.

</aside>
<div class="p1-main">

## What you'll do

You did it by hand last time. Now you'll have the **AI Assistant** build the same kind of report from a ready-made prompt — you answer a few questions, review what it proposes, and it writes the slides into your deck.

<h2 class="step-h"><span class="step-n">1</span><span>Create the slide deck</span></h2>

Open the **Slide decks** tab → **+ Create slide deck** → give it a name (e.g. *Disruption Report — {country}*). This empty deck is where the AI will write.

</div>
</div>

<h2 class="step-h"><span class="step-n">2</span><span>Open the AI Assistant and pick the prompt</span></h2>

Open the **AI Assistant**. If you're already in a conversation, start a **new** one.

Open the **Prompt Library**, scroll to the bottom, and choose the prompt for your case:

- **Prompt 1 — FASTR Disruptions Report** — national level
- **Prompt 4 — Subnational Disruptions Report** — by region / district

The prompt text appears in the input field. Read it.

> **What the prompt does.** It's a set of instructions telling the AI how to build the report: ask you for basic details, look up which indicators your country has, group them into categories for you to confirm, then build the report slide by slide. The structure, layout, and methodology wording are standardised — only your country's indicators, period, and findings change.

<h2 class="step-h"><span class="step-n">3</span><span>Run it and answer the AI's questions</span></h2>

Click **Run in current chat**. Before it builds anything, the AI asks the driver a few things — type the answers in the chat:

- **Analysis period** — start and end date (e.g. *January 2023 to September 2025*)
- **Cover subtitle** — pick one it offers (e.g. *Q3 2025*) or type your own
- **Indicator groupings** — it discovers your indicators and proposes categories (next step)
- **Mortality indicators** — it asks separately whether to include these (e.g. maternal deaths, stillbirths), since they read differently

<h2 class="step-h"><span class="step-n">4</span><span>Review the indicator groupings</span></h2>

The AI reads your country's data, groups the available indicators, and asks: *do these make sense?* Decide as a team:

- ✓ Do the groups fit your country?
- ✎ Merge, split, or rename any?
- ✕ Any indicators to leave out?

<p class="meta-line">Typical groups: Antenatal care (ANC1, ANC4) · Deliveries & postnatal · Immunization (BCG, Penta1, Penta3) · Family planning · Malaria · HIV/TB · Outpatient · Nutrition</p>

<h2 class="step-h"><span class="step-n">5</span><span>Confirm — and let it build</span></h2>

Reply with your final groupings (plus any changes and exclusions). The AI then writes the report into your deck: cover, methodology, then one slide per group — each with a **disruption chart** (actual vs expected service volume) and a **written interpretation**.

> **Let it finish.** Generating all slides takes about **5–10 minutes** (longer on a weak connection). Don't click a slide while it's working — that interrupts it. Wait until every slide is created, then review.

## Check yourself

- A new deck exists with cover + methodology + one slide per group
- Each analysis slide has a chart **and** an interpretation
- The country, period, and subtitle are correct

## What's next

Don't trust it yet. The next handout — **Verify the output** — is a checklist for reading every slide before you use the report.
