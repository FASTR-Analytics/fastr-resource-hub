# Peer review and present

<p class="meta-line"><strong>Activity</strong> · <strong>Disruption Report</strong> · <strong>~40 min</strong></p>

<div class="p1-grid">
<aside class="p1-sidebar">

<p class="sb-label">Before you start</p>

- ☐ Your report is built, verified, and refined
- ☐ You're paired with another country team
- ☐ Read theirs as a decision-maker would

</aside>
<div class="p1-main">

## What you'll do

Swap reports with another team and read theirs cold — the way someone who wasn't in the room will. Then give each other clear, useful feedback.

<h2 class="step-h"><span class="step-n">1</span><span>Swap and read</span></h2>

Pair up with another country team and swap reports. Read theirs as a fresh reader:

- Is the **main message** clear?
- Is it easy to follow?
- Would a **decision-maker** know what to do next?

</div>
</div>

---

<div class="brand-line"><span class="rule"></span><img src="../../../resources/logos/FASTR_Primary_01_FullName.png" alt="FASTR" height="28"></div>

<h2 class="step-h"><span class="step-n">2</span><span>Prepare feedback</span></h2>

For the report you reviewed, note:

- **2 things that work well**
- **2 suggestions to improve**

<div class="answer-box"></div>

<h2 class="step-h"><span class="step-n">3</span><span>Present back</span></h2>

- Each team presents their section (~5 min)
- Share the key strengths and top suggestions for the report you reviewed

**Group discussion:** What worked well? What was challenging? Any feedback on the approach?

## Check yourself

- You gave the other team 2 strengths + 2 suggestions
- You captured the feedback on your own report
- You know the one change you'll make next
