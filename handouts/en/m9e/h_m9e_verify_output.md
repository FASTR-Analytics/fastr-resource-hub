# Verify the AI output

<div class="p1-main">

## What you'll do

Read every slide before you use the report. The AI is fast, but it can produce confident-sounding mistakes — wrong period, a chart that didn't load, an interpretation that overstates the trend. You sign off on it, so you check it.

Work through both checklists below as a team.

## General — every report

- ☐ All slides generated — none missing
- ☐ Country name correct and consistent
- ☐ Time period correct on every chart
- ☐ No placeholder text left (e.g. `[COUNTRY]`)
- ☐ The back page is the very last slide

## The disruptions report

- ☐ Cover slide has the correct country, subtitle, and date
- ☐ Indicator groupings match what you confirmed
- ☐ Each group has its own analysis slide
- ☐ Charts are loading and showing data
- ☐ Interpretations match the charts
- ☐ Slide titles are **findings**, not just indicator names

> **Read every interpretation aloud, together.** Does anything sound wrong or exaggerated? A chart shows *actual vs expected service volume* — if the words say "sharp decline" but the line barely dips, fix the words. *(For what "expected" means, see the methodology recap: Service use.)*

## Check yourself

- Every slide has been read by at least one person
- Anything wrong is noted for the next step
- The numbers and the words agree

## What's next

**Refine** — apply your formatting and, if useful, add regional or data-quality sections with Prompt 2 and Prompt 3.
