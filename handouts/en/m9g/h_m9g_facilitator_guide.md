# Facilitator guide — FASTR Quiz

<p class="meta-line"><strong>Facilitator guide</strong> · <strong>FASTR Quiz</strong></p>

## About this activity

A short, team-based knowledge check on FASTR fundamentals — the framework, the data-quality dimensions, and indicator definitions. Teams may use the AI Assistant to find answers, which doubles as light practice with the tool.

**One participant sheet.** **~30–45 min.** Best as an energiser or a recap near the end.

## How to run it

- Put participants in **teams**; one answer sheet per team.
- Run it as a race or a relaxed discussion — your call. Allow the AI Assistant; finding the answer with it is part of the point.
- After each question, reveal the answer and give the one-line reason. Let teams debate before you confirm.
- Keep it light. The goal is to surface and settle misconceptions, not to grade.

## Answer key

**1. What does FASTR stand for? → B**
*Frequent Assessments and System Tools for Resilience.* Not a "Framework / Registry" — FASTR is about rapid, repeated assessment, not a one-off audit.

**2. The 3 data quality dimensions? → C**
*Outliers, completeness, internal consistency* — the three checks the DQA module runs. (A and B mix in terms from other quality frameworks.)

**3. The 4 technical pillars? → B**
*RMNCAH-N service-use analysis (HMIS), facility phone surveys, household/client phone surveys, rapid qualitative studies.*

**4. 200 ANC1 but 350 ANC4 → A**
*Consistency.* You can't have more 4th visits than 1st visits — ANC1 must be ≥ ANC4, so the numbers can't both be right.

**5. ANC1 coverage target population → A**
*Pregnant women* (expected pregnancies) — the denominator for first antenatal visit coverage.
