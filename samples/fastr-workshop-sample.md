# What is FASTR?

An approach to catalyzing continuous **analyze, learn, strengthen, act** cycles to drive the systematic use of timely data for decision making.

- **Analyze** — timely PHC and RMNCAH-N data
- **Learn** — from the data, to prioritize health-systems gaps
- **Strengthen** — within-country M&E capacity and data quality
- **Act** — translate data into action for stronger PHC outcomes

> Year 1 → Year 5: continuous data use, every cycle.

---

# Why monthly facility-level data?

Monthly facility data lets you spot **service disruptions within a single quarter**, rather than waiting for an annual report to confirm what every analyst already suspected.

1. **Granularity** — district patterns surface when national averages hide them
2. **Cadence** — anomalies are flagged in weeks, not years
3. **Comparability** — same indicators every month make trends honest
4. **Decision-ready** — outputs land in time to redirect supervision and supply

# Three signals to track every month

- **Completeness** — share of facilities reporting on time
- **Consistency** — month-on-month change against expected band
- **Plausibility** — value within physically possible range
- **Concordance** — agreement with parallel sources (HFA, surveys)

When all four hold, the next conversation is about *intervention*, not data.
